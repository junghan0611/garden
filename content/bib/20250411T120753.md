Bionic reading experience with Zotero. Bionic Reading is a reading method designed to make it easier and faster to comprehend text by guiding the reader's eyes through bolded initial letters of words. This technique emphasizes the beginning of each word, allowing the brain to "fill in" the rest of the word and phrase intuitively, leveraging cognitive shortcuts. As shown in this sentence, the bolded initial letters of each word are used to guide the reader's eyes through the text, making it easier to comprehend.
