## Personal knowledge management 개인 지식 관리 {#personal-knowledge-management-개인-지식-관리}

(“Personal Knowledge Management 개인 지식 관리” 2025)

Personal knowledge management (PKM) is a process of collecting information that a person uses to gather, classify, store, search, retrieve and share knowledge in their daily activities (Grundspenkis 2007) and the way in which these processes support work activities (Wright 2005). It is a response to the idea that knowledge workers need to be responsible for their own growth and learning (Smedley 2009). It is a bottom-up approach to knowledge management (KM) (Pollard 2008).

## 참고 {#참고}


### 위키피디아 {#위키피디아}

**Personal knowledge management** ( **PKM**) is a process of collecting information that a person uses to gather, classify, store, search, retrieve and share [knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge) in their daily activities ([Grundspenkis 2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFGrundspenkis2007)) and the way in which these processes support work activities ([Wright 2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFWright2005)). It is a response to the idea that [knowledge workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_worker) need to be responsible for their own growth and learning ([Smedley 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFSmedley2009)). It is a bottom-up approach to [knowledge management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_management) (KM) ([Pollard 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFPollard2008)).

**개인 지식 관리** ( **PKM**)는 개인이 수집, 분류, 저장, 검색하는 데 사용하는 정보를 수집하는 프로세스입니다, [지식](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge)를 검색하고 공유하는 일상 활동([Grundspenkis 2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFGrundspenkis2007))과 이러한 프로세스가 업무 활동을 지원하는 방식([Wright 2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFWright2005))을 말합니다. 이는 지식 근로자가 자신의 성장과 학습에 대해 책임을 져야 한다는 생각에 대한 반응입니다([Smedley 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_worker)). 이는 [지식 관리(KM)에 대한 상향식 접근 방식입니다(](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_management)[Pollard 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFPollard2008)).


#### History and background역사 및 배경 {#history-and-background-역사-및-배경}

Although as early as 1998 Davenport wrote on the importance to worker productivity of understanding individual knowledge processes (cited in [Zhang 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFZhang2009)), the term _personal knowledge management_ appears to be relatively new. Its origin can be traced in a working paper by [Frand &amp; Hixon (1999)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFFrandHixon1999). 1998년 초에 Davenport는 개인 지식 프로세스를 이해하는 것이 작업자의 생산성에 미치는 중요성에 대해 썼지만([Zhang 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFZhang2009)에서 인용), '개인 지식 관리'라는 용어는 비교적 최근에 등장한 것으로 보입니다. 이 용어의 기원은 [Frand &amp; Hixon (1999)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFFrandHixon1999)의 작업 논문에서 찾을 수 있습니다.

PKM integrates [personal information management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_information_management) (PIM), focused on individual skills, with [knowledge management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_management) (KM) in addition to input from a variety of disciplines such as [cognitive psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_psychology), [management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management) and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) ([Pauleen 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFPauleen2009)). From an organizational perspective, understanding of the field has developed in light of expanding knowledge about human [cognitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive) capabilities and the permeability of organizational boundaries. From a [metacognitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacognitive) perspective, it compares various modalities within human cognition as to their competence and efficacy ([Sheridan 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFSheridan2008)). It is an underresearched area ([Pauleen 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFPauleen2009)). More recently, research has been conducted to help understand "the potential role of [Web 2.0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_2.0) technologies for harnessing and managing personal knowledge" ([Razmerita, Kirchner &amp; Sudzina 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFRazmeritaKirchnerSudzina2009)). The [Great Resignation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Resignation) has expanded the category of [knowledge workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_workers) and is predicted to increase demand for personal knowledge management in the future ([Serenko 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFSerenko2023)).

PKM은 개인 역량에 초점을 맞춘 [개인 정보 관리(PIM)를](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_information_management) [지식 관리(KM)와 통합하고 여기에](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_management) [인지 심리학 등 다양한 학문의 의견을 더합니다,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_psychology) [경영학](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management) 및 [철학](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy)([Pauleen 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFPauleen2009)). 조직의 관점에서 볼 때, 인간의 [인지적](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive) 능력과 조직 경계의 침투성에 대한 지식이 확대됨에 따라 이 분야에 대한 이해가 발전했습니다. [메타인지적](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacognitive) 관점에서 인간의 인지 능력과 효능에 대한 다양한 양식을 비교합니다([Sheridan 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFSheridan2008)). 이 분야는 아직 연구가 덜 이루어진 분야입니다([Pauleen 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFPauleen2009)). 최근에는 "개인 지식을 활용하고 관리하기 위한 [웹 2.0 기술의 잠재적 역할"에 대한 이해를 돕기 위한 연구가 수행되었습니다(](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_2.0)[Razmerita, Kirchner &amp; Sudzina 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFRazmeritaKirchnerSudzina2009)). [대퇴직](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Resignation)은 [지식 근로자](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_workers)의 범주를 확장했으며 향후 개인 지식 관리에 대한 수요가 증가할 것으로 예측됩니다([Serenko 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFSerenko2023)).


#### Models모델 {#models-모델}

[Dorsey (2001)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFDorsey2001) identified information retrieval, assessment and evaluation, organization, analysis, presentation, security, and collaboration as essential to PKM (cited in [Zhang 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFZhang2009)).

[Dorsey(2001)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFDorsey2001)는 정보 검색, 평가 및 평가, 조직, 분석, 프레젠테이션, 보안 및 협업을 PKM의 필수 요소로 확인했습니다([Zhang 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFZhang2009)에서 인용).

Wright's model involves four interrelated domains: analytical, information, social, and learning. The analytical domain involves competencies such as interpretation, envisioning, application, creation, and contextualization. The information dimension comprises the sourcing, assessment, organization, aggregation, and communication of information. The social dimension involves finding and collaborating with people, the development of both close networks and extended networks, and dialogue. The learning dimension entails expanding pattern recognition and sensemaking capabilities, reflection, development of new knowledge, improvement of skills, and extension to others. This model stresses the importance of both bonding and bridging networks ([Wright 2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFWright2007)).

라이트의 모델에는 분석, 정보, 사회, 학습의 네 가지 상호 연관된 영역이 포함됩니다. 분석 영역에는 해석, 구상, 적용, 창조, 맥락화 등의 역량이 포함됩니다. 정보 차원은 정보의 소싱, 평가, 조직, 집계 및 커뮤니케이션으로 구성됩니다. 사회적 차원은 사람을 찾고 협력하는 것, 긴밀한 네트워크와 확장된 네트워크의 개발, 대화를 포함합니다. 학습 차원은 패턴 인식 및 감각 형성 능력의 확장, 성찰, 새로운 지식의 개발, 기술 향상, 다른 사람에게로의 확장을 수반합니다. 이 모델은 네트워크의 결합과 연결 모두의 중요성을 강조합니다([Wright 2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFWright2007)).

In Nonaka and Takeuchi's [SECI model of knowledge dimensions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SECI_model_of_knowledge_dimensions) (see under [knowledge management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_management)), knowledge can be tacit or explicit, with the interaction of the two resulting in new knowledge ([Nonaka &amp; Takeuchi 1995](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFNonakaTakeuchi1995)). Smedley has developed a PKM model based on Nonaka and colleagues' model in which an expert provides direction while a [community of practice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_of_practice) provides support for personal knowledge creation ([Smedley 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFSmedley2009)). Trust is central to knowledge sharing in this model. Nonaka has returned to his earlier work in an attempt to further develop his ideas about knowledge creation ([Nonaka &amp; von Krogh 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFNonakavon_Krogh2009))

노나카와 타케우치의 [지식 차원에 대한 SECI 모델](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SECI_model_of_knowledge_dimensions)([지식 관리](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_management) 아래 참조)에서 지식은 암묵적이거나 명시적일 수 있으며, 두 지식의 상호작용으로 새로운 지식을 창출합니다([Nonaka &amp; Takeuchi 1995](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFNonakaTakeuchi1995)). 스메들리는 노나카와 동료들의 모델을 기반으로 전문가가 방향을 제시하고 [실무 커뮤니티가 개인 지식 창출을 지원하는 PKM 모델을 개발했습니다(](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_of_practice)[Smedley 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFSmedley2009)). 이 모델에서 신뢰는 지식 공유의 핵심입니다. 노나카는 지식 창출에 대한 자신의 아이디어를 더욱 발전시키기 위해 이전 작업으로 돌아갔습니다([Nonaka &amp; von Krogh 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFNonakavon_Krogh2009)).

Personal knowledge management can also be viewed along two main dimensions, personal [knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge) and personal [management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management) ([Zhang 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFZhang2009)). Zhang has developed a model of PKM in relation to organizational knowledge management (OKM) that considers two axes of knowledge properties and management perspectives, either organizational or personal. These aspects of organizational and personal knowledge are interconnected through the OAPI process (organizationalize, aggregate, personalize, and individualize), whereby organizational knowledge is personalized and individualized, and personal knowledge is aggregated and operationalized as organizational knowledge ([Zhang 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFZhang2009)). 개인 지식 관리는 개인 [지식](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge)과 개인 [관리](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management)의 두 가지 주요 차원으로 볼 수도 있습니다([Zhang 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFZhang2009)). 장은 조직적 또는 개인적 지식 속성과 관리 관점이라는 두 가지 축을 고려하는 조직 지식 관리(OKM)와 관련된 PKM 모델을 개발했습니다. 조직 지식과 개인 지식의 이러한 측면은 OAPI 프로세스(조직화, 집계, 개인화, 개별화)를 통해 상호 연결되어 조직 지식은 개인화 및 개별화되고, 개인 지식은 조직 지식으로 집계 및 운영됩니다([Zhang 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFZhang2009)).


#### Criticism비평 {#criticism-비평}

It is not clear whether PKM is anything more than a new wrapper around [personal information management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_information_management) (PIM). William Jones argued that only personal information as a tangible resource can be managed, whereas personal knowledge cannot ([Jones 2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFJones2010)). [Dave Snowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Snowden) has asserted that most individuals cannot manage their knowledge in the traditional sense of "managing" and has advocated thinking in terms of [sensemaking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensemaking) rather than PKM ([Snowden &amp; Pauleen 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFSnowdenPauleen2008)). Knowledge is not solely an individual product—it emerges through connections, dialog, and social interaction (see [Sociology of knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology_of_knowledge)). However, in Wright's model, PKM involves the application to problem-solving of analytical, information, social, and learning dimensions, which are interrelated ([Wright 2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFWright2007)), and so is inherently social. PKM이 [개인 정보 관리(PIM)를 새롭게 포장한 것에 지나지 않는지 여부는 명확하지 않습니다. 윌리엄 존스는 유형 자원으로서의 개인 정보만 관리할 수 있지만 개인 지식은 관리할 수 없다고 주장했습니다(](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_information_management)[Jones 2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFJones2010)). [Dave Snowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Snowden)는 대부분의 개인은 전통적인 의미의 '관리'로는 자신의 지식을 관리할 수 없다고 주장하며 PKM이 아닌 [감각 형성의 관점에서 사고할 것을 주장했습니다(](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensemaking)[Snowden &amp; Pauleen 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFSnowdenPauleen2008)). 지식은 개별적인 산물이 아니라 연결, 대화, 사회적 상호작용을 통해 생겨납니다([지식 사회학](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology_of_knowledge) 참조). 그러나 Wright의 모델에서 PKM은 분석, 정보, 사회, 학습 차원을 문제 해결에 적용하는 것으로 상호 연관되어 있으므로([Wright 2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFWright2007)) 본질적으로 사회적 성격을 띠고 있습니다.

An aim of PKM is "helping individuals to be more effective in personal, organizational and social environments" ([Pauleen 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFPauleen2009), p.221), often through the use of technology such as networking software. It has been argued, however, that equation of PKM with technology has limited the value and utility of the concept (e.g., [Pollard 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFPollard2008), [Snowden &amp; Pauleen 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFSnowdenPauleen2008)). PKM의 목표는 "개인이 개인, 조직 및 사회적 환경에서 더 효과적으로 일할 수 있도록 돕는 것"([Pauleen 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFPauleen2009), 221쪽)이며, 종종 네트워킹 소프트웨어와 같은 기술을 사용합니다. 그러나 PKM을 기술과 동일시하는 것은 개념의 가치와 유용성을 제한한다는 주장도 있습니다(예: [Pollard 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFPollard2008), [Snowden &amp; Pauleen 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFSnowdenPauleen2008)).

In 2012, Mohamed Chatti introduced the personal knowledge network (PKN) model to KM as an alternative perspective on PKM, based on the concepts of a personal knowledge network and knowledge ecology ([Chatti 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFChatti2012)). 2012년 모하메드 차티는 개인 지식 네트워크와 지식 생태학의 개념을 바탕으로 개인 지식 네트워크(PKN) 모델을 PKM에 대한 대안적 관점으로 KM에 도입했습니다([Chatti 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_knowledge_management#CITEREFChatti2012)).


#### Skills기술 {#skills-기술}

Skills associated with personal knowledge management include: 개인 지식 관리와 관련된 기술에는 다음이 포함됩니다:

-   [Collaboration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaboration) skills. Coordination, [synchronization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synchronization), [experimentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimentation), [cooperation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooperation) and [design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design). [협업](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaboration) 기술. 조정, [동기화](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synchronization), [실험](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimentation), [협력](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooperation) 및 [디자인](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design) 기술입니다.
-   Communication skills. [Perception](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perception), [intuition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intuition_(knowledge)), [expression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emotional_expression), [visualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_image) and [interpretation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpretation_(logic)). 의사소통 능력. [지각](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perception), [직관](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intuition_(knowledge)), [표현](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emotional_expression), [시각화](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_image) 및 [해석](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpretation_(logic))이 있습니다.
-   Creative skills. Imagination, [pattern recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pattern_recognition), appreciation, innovation, inference. Understanding of [complex adaptive systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_adaptive_systems). 창의력. 상상력, [패턴 인식](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pattern_recognition), 감상, 혁신, 추론. [복잡한 적응 시스템에 대한 이해.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_adaptive_systems)
-   [Information literacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_literacy). Understanding what information is important and how to find unknown information. [정보 리터러시](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_literacy). 어떤 정보가 중요하고 알려지지 않은 정보를 찾는 방법을 이해하는 능력입니다.
-   Manage learning. Manage how and when the individual learns. 학습을 관리합니다. 개인이 학습하는 방법과 시기를 관리합니다.
-   [Networking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_network) with others. Knowing what your network of people knows. Knowing who might have additional knowledge and resources to help you [다른 사람들과의 네트워킹](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_network). 인맥이 무엇을 알고 있는지 파악하기. 도움을 줄 수 있는 추가 지식과 리소스가 있는 사람 파악하기
-   Organizational skills. Personal [librarianship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Librarian). Personal [categorization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorization) and [taxonomies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxonomy_(general)). 조직 기술. 개인 [사서직](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Librarian). 개인 [분류](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorization) 및 [분류학](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxonomy_(general)).
-   Reflection. [Continuous improvement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaizen) on how the individual operates. 반영. [개인 운영 방식에 대한 지속적인 개선](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaizen).
-   Researching, [canvassing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canvassing), paying [attention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention), [interviewing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interviewing) and [observational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observation) "cultural anthropology" skills 조사, [조사](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canvassing), [주의 집중](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention), [인터뷰](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interviewing), [관찰](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observation) "문화인류학" 기술 습득


#### Tools도구 {#tools-도구}

Some organizations are introducing PKM "systems" with some or all four components:

일부 조직에서는 네 가지 구성 요소 중 일부 또는 전부를 포함하는 PKM '시스템'을 도입하고 있습니다:

-   [Content management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_management): taxonomy processes and desktop search tools that enable employees to subscribe to, find, organize and publish information that resides on their desktops [콘텐츠 관리](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_management): 직원들이 데스크톱에 있는 정보를 구독, 검색, 정리 및 게시할 수 있는 분류 프로세스 및 데스크톱 검색 도구입니다.
-   Just-in-time [canvassing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canvassing): templates and e-mail canvassing lists that enable people to identify and connect with the appropriate experts and expertise quickly and effectively 적시에 [조사](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canvassing): 사람들이 적절한 전문가와 전문 지식을 빠르고 효과적으로 식별하고 연결할 수 있는 템플릿 및 이메일 조사 목록입니다.
-   Knowledge harvesting: software tools that automatically collect appropriate knowledge residing on subject matter experts' hard drives 지식 하베스팅: 주제별 전문가의 하드 드라이브에 있는 적절한 지식을 자동으로 수집하는 소프트웨어 도구
-   Personal productivity improvement: knowledge fairs and 101 training sessions to help each employee make more effective personal use of the knowledge, learning, and technology resources available in the context of their work 개인 생산성 향상: 지식 박람회 및 101개의 교육 세션을 통해 각 직원이 업무 맥락에서 사용 가능한 지식, 학습 및 기술 리소스를 보다 효과적으로 개인적으로 활용할 수 있도록 지원합니다.

PKM has also been linked to these tools: PKM은 다음 도구와도 연결되어 있습니다:

-   Email, calendars, task managers 이메일, 캘린더, 작업 관리자
-   Knowledge logs (k-logs)지식 로그(K-로그)
-   [Social bookmarking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_bookmarking) and [enterprise bookmarking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enterprise_bookmarking) [소셜 북마크](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_bookmarking) 및 [엔터프라이즈 북마크](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enterprise_bookmarking)
-   [Virtual assistants가상 비서](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_assistant_(occupation))
-   [Wikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiki), including [personal wikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_wiki) and [semantic wikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semantic_wiki) [위키](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiki)([개인 위키](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_wiki) 및 [시맨틱 위키](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semantic_wiki) 포함)를 포함합니다.
-   [Web annotations웹 주석](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_annotation)

Other useful tools include stories and [narrative inquiry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narrative_inquiry), [decision support systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_support_system), various kinds of [node–link diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_(discrete_mathematics)) (such as [argument maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argument_map), [mind maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_map), [concept maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concept_map)), and similar [information visualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_visualization) techniques. Individuals use these tools to capture ideas, expertise, experience, opinions or thoughts, and this "voicing" will encourage cognitive diversity and promote free exchanges away from a centralized policed knowledge repository. The goal is to facilitate knowledge sharing and personal content management. 다른 유용한 도구로는 스토리 및 [내러티브 문의](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narrative_inquiry), [의사 결정 지원 시스템](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_support_system), 다양한 종류의 [노드-링크 다이어그램](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_(discrete_mathematics))(예: [논증 맵](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argument_map)) 및 이와 유사한 [마인드 맵 등이 있습니다,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_map) [마인드 맵](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_map), [개념 맵](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concept_map)) 및 유사한 [정보 시각화](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_visualization) 기법을 사용합니다. 개인은 이러한 도구를 사용하여 아이디어, 전문 지식, 경험, 의견 또는 생각을 포착하며, 이러한 '목소리 내기'는 인지적 다양성을 장려하고 중앙집중식 정책 지식 저장소에서 벗어나 자유로운 교류를 촉진합니다
