## Separate Paragraph 단락 {#separate-paragraph}

```markdown { linenos=false,filename="page.md" }
$$F(\omega) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t) e^{-j\omega t} \, dt$$
```

will be rendered as:

\\[F(\omega) = \int\_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t) e^{-j\omega t} \\, dt\\]
